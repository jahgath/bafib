# 01 Product Vision

## Problem
Small business owners run everything from their heads, paper, WhatsApp and spreadsheets. They lose time and money to missed follow-ups, double bookings, stock-outs, late invoices and manual repetition. They can't afford consultants and are overwhelmed by software choices and jargon.

## Solution
A guided conversation that acts like a friendly consultant. It learns how the business works, finds the bottlenecks, and recommends practical fixes using free or cheap tools.

## Personas
- **Priya, salon owner (3 staff):** books by phone and WhatsApp, forgets reminders, loses no-shows. Uses her phone only.
- **Marco, small restaurant/cafe owner:** tracks stock on paper, overorders, struggles with staff rosters.
- **Dan, solo tradesperson (plumber/electrician):** quotes by text, invoices late, chases payments.
- **Aisha, online home-based seller:** orders arrive via Instagram DMs, manual tracking, inconsistent replies.

## Principles
1. **Accessible first:** large text, voice, big buttons, no jargon, works on a cheap phone and slow data.
2. **One question at a time**, short and concrete, with examples.
3. **Never make the user feel judged.** Frame problems as common and fixable.
4. **Cheap or free beats powerful.** Recommend the simplest tool that works.
5. **Actionable over comprehensive.** A few clear steps, not a 40-page audit.
6. **Trust:** explain why we ask, keep data private, let users skip or stop anytime.

## Goals
- 80%+ of users who start finish the conversation (target 15-20 min max).
- Report is understood without help (tested with real owners).
- Users act on at least one recommendation within 2 weeks.

## Non-goals (v1)
- Integrating directly with the user's tools or automating setup
- Multi-language support
- Deep accounting, legal or tax advice
- Team or multi-user accounts

## Success metrics
Completion rate, time to complete, report helpfulness rating (1-5), recommendations clicked, follow-up return rate, voice vs chat usage split.
