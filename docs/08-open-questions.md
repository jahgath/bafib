# 08 Open Questions

| # | Question | Why it matters | Options / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Monetization** | Shapes tool recommendations (affiliate bias) and the report depth | Free, freemium, paid, B2B2C |
| 2 | **Target region** | Affects tool availability, currency, payments, WhatsApp usage | Start with the user's home market? |
| 3 | **Voice provider** | Latency, accent accuracy, cost | Web Speech API, Deepgram, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Google |
| 4 | **Languages after English** | Biggest accessibility lever for the target group | Hindi, Spanish, others; decide from beta demand |
| 5 | **Compliance and data retention** | GDPR/other laws, voice recordings | Retention period, region for data storage |
| 6 | **Catalog owner** | Pricing and free tiers change often | Who verifies quarterly? |
| 7 | **Human advisor in the loop?** | Quality and trust at launch | Review reports in beta, optional paid human review later |
| 8 | **Brand name and tone** | Trust with non-technical users | Needs naming and voice work |
| 9 | **Industry-specific journeys** | Depth vs breadth | Start generic, add verticals from data |
| 10 | **Success definition for beta** | Go/no-go decision | Completion %, helpfulness, actions taken |
